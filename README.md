# Pesqueiro.GG

Pesqueiro.GG is a gamified social network for recreational anglers in Brazil. Every catch you log fills a sticker in your species album, moves your group's season ranking, and becomes part of your region's fishing story.

> **Status:** in development for [Hack Brasil 2026](https://hackbrasil.com), track *Cultura & Economia Criativa*. Nothing is deployed yet. This page lists what's planned; it says so explicitly when a feature is working.

## Why Pesqueiro.GG

Recreational fishing is one of Brazil's most widespread social traditions, but the culture it produces lives in WhatsApp groups and generic feeds. Catch stories, regional species, techniques and friendly rivalries vanish in the scroll, can't be searched, and never reach beyond the group.

Pesqueiro.GG turns each catch into a lasting, structured record: species, place, technique and photo. Gamification gives anglers a reason to document every trip:

- **Álbum de Espécies:** a sticker album where each new species you catch fills a slot. Rare and regional species are worth more.
- **Group seasons and challenges:** quarterly rankings among friends, and monthly challenges such as "most species in November".
- **Career levels:** progress from *Jovem Aprendiz* to *CEO dos Mares*.

XP rewards variety and documentation, not volume, and exact fishing spots are never shown to anyone but the person who caught the fish.

## What's planned for the first release

| Area | What you can do | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Groups | Create a group, invite friends from WhatsApp, see the group's feed | Planned |
| Catches | Log a catch with photos, get an AI species suggestion, choose who sees it | Planned |
| Gamification | Earn XP, fill the album, level up, compete in group seasons and challenges | Planned |
| Social | React, comment, share tips and trip stories, follow anglers, browse public catches | Planned |
| Trust and privacy | City-level places only, photo metadata removed, reports and moderation | Planned |

Progress is tracked on the [project board](https://github.com/orgs/Pesqueiro-GG/projects/1).
