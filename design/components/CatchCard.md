# CatchCard

A catch in a feed: photo first, then the species, facts, the approximate place and the story.

- The photo is 4:3 and fills the card width. Without a photo, the sea placeholder with the fish silhouette shows.
- Facts sit on one line in this order: weight, length, type of water, "Solto" when released. Leave out what's missing.
- The place is always the city and state ("Igaratá, SP"), or a pay-to-fish lake's name. Never coordinates.
- "Foto conferida" (photo checked) appears only when the server set the proof level to photo + AI match.
- "Figurinha nova" appears when this catch filled a sticker.
- The reaction is "Fisgou" with the fishing-hook icon; it turns `bobber-text` once you've reacted.
- Consumer provides every text already formatted for the viewer's language ("2,4 kg" in Portuguese, "2.4 kg" in English).
