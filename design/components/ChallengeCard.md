# ChallengeCard

A challenge in a list: who runs it, the rule, time left and your position.

- Group challenges show the group icon; official challenges show the globe and a gold edge.
- Official challenges show the "Foto + IA" chip, because they need a photo-checked catch.
- The rule is one sentence people can check themselves: what counts, which fish, which water.
- Consumer provides: `title`, `rule`, `ends`, `participants`, and optionally `myPosition`, `scope`, `proof`, `lang`.
