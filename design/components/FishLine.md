# FishLine

Progress drawn as a fishing line, with the bobber at the current point.

- Use it for progress toward the next level and toward a goal challenge. Not for loading.
- The line already cast is solid `water`; what's left is dashed `line-strong`.
- Always pair it with a label that says what's left: "Faltam 180 XP para Pescador Sênior".
- Consumer provides: `value`, `max`, and `label` (also the accessible name).
