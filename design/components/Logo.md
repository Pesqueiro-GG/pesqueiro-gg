# Logo

The bobber mark, alone or with the Pesqueiro.GG wordmark.

- Use `variant="full"` (default) in the app header and on the landing page; `variant="mark"` where space is tight (loading, small brand spots).
- `size` is the mark's height in px; the wordmark scales with it. Keep it at 24px or more.
- The wordmark is set live in the `display` family: "Pesqueiro" in `ink`, ".GG" in `water`. Never retype it in another font.
- The bobber dips on hover, like a fish biting. The motion stops under reduced motion.
- For the app icon, favicon and link previews use `assets/Logos/pesqueiro-mark.svg`, which adds the water square.
- Consumer provides: `size` and `variant`. Don't recolor the bobber; its cream and band are fixed in both themes (`mark-cream`, `mark-band`).
