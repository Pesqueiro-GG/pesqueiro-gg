# PromotionBanner

The level-up moment, announced as a job promotion with the new career title.

- Show it once, after the catch that crossed the level threshold: on the reward screen, or at the top of the feed.
- The copy is gender-neutral: "Promoção! Seu novo cargo é" and "Promotion! Your new position is".
- The button closes the banner and carries the joke ("Aceitar o cargo").
- Consumer provides: `rank` (the title for the new level), `level`, `onAccept`, `lang`.
