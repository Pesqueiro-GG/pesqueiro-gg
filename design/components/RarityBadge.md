# RarityBadge

The species tier as a word plus a color: Comum, Incomum, Raro, Épico, or Protegida.

- The word always shows. The four tier colors differ mostly by hue, so color alone never carries the tier.
- The tier comes from the species catalog, never from the catch. A protected species shows `protegida` instead of a tier and gives no rarity bonus.
- Use it beside species names on catch cards, stickers and species pages. Not in rankings.
- Consumer provides: `tier`, and `lang="en"` for the English labels.
